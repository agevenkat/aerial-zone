## Aagile Kidz preschool program

A playful introduction to movement for preschool children. Classes use games, simple shapes and rolls to help little ones enjoy being active and grow in confidence.

<ul class="ticks">
<li>Balance, coordination and body awareness</li>
<li>Listening, taking turns and following simple instructions</li>
<li>A fun, positive first experience of sport</li>
</ul>

## Motor skill development program

Focused on the basic movement skills young children need for any sport: running, jumping, balancing, climbing, rolling and catching. A strong foundation now makes every later skill easier.

<ul class="ticks">
<li>Gross motor skills and coordination</li>
<li>Strength and flexibility suited to young bodies</li>
<li>Confidence through safe, guided challenges</li>
</ul>

## Elite training program

For committed gymnasts who want to train seriously and compete. Coaching focuses on advanced technique, routines and the conditioning needed for competition.

<ul class="ticks">
<li>Coaching from experienced, certified coaches and judges</li>
<li>Routine building and competition preparation</li>
<li>Strength, flexibility and mental focus</li>
</ul>

</div>

## Recreation gymnastics for schools

We bring gymnastics into schools as part of their sports program, helping students stay active, build fitness and discover the sport.

<ul class="ticks">
<li>Structured sessions run by our coaches</li>
<li>Suitable for a range of ages and abilities</li>
<li>A route into further training for interested students</li>
</ul>
