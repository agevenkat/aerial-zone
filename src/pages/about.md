# Creating champions for life

<p class="lead">Aerial Zone is a school of gymnastics in Gottigere, Bengaluru. We believe sport is not just about winning and losing. It prepares children for the future in many parts of life.</p>

</section>

<div class="split">
<div>

<span class="eyebrow">Our story</span>

## Two decades in grassroots sport

Aerial Zone is brought to you by True Sport. Our team has been involved in grassroots sport for more than twenty years, and True Sport was officially registered in 2009. Since then, we have worked with thousands of children through community programs, school programs and our academy.

Our main focus is grassroots training in gymnastics, a football program, and motor skill development for toddlers. Everything we do comes from one belief: sport can play a big part in shaping and developing young people in India, and in helping children build healthier lifestyles.

## The people behind Aerial Zone

<p class="lead">Our core team brings together decades of experience in coaching, judging, sports management and business.</p>

### Dr. Anuradha K Namashivaya

A sports management professional with a medical background and more than twenty years in the sports industry, with experience at national and international sports organisations and events. ### Mr. R Chethan

Holds the FIG Level 3 International Diploma in Men's Artistic Gymnastics (MAG) and the FIG Level 1 International Diploma in Women's Artistic Gymnastics (WAG).
