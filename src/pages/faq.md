# Questions, answered

<p class="lead">Here are answers to the questions parents and students ask us most. If yours is not here, just get in touch.</p>

</section>

<section>
<div class="faq">
<details open>
<summary>What ages do you teach?</summary>

We teach children from age 3, teens and adults. Classes are grouped into ages 3–7, 8–12, 12–18 and 18+, so everyone trains with others at a similar stage.

</details>
<details>
<summary>What programs do you offer?</summary>

We offer four programs: the Aagile Kidz preschool program, a motor skill development program, an elite training program for gymnasts who want to compete, and recreation gymnastics for schools. See our [programs page](/services/) for details.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Who are the coaches?</summary>

Our core team includes Dr. Anuradha K Namashivaya, an international judge and coach, and Mr. R Chethan, who holds FIG international coaching diplomas in men's and women's artistic gymnastics and is NSNIS certified by the Sports Authority of India. Read more on our [About page](/about/).

</details>
<details>
<summary>Why is gymnastics good for young children?</summary>

Gymnastics builds confidence through safe risk-taking. Children burn off energy, develop strength, balance and coordination, and learn to focus on completing a routine.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Do your students take part in competitions?</summary>

Yes. Our gymnasts have won medals at national level, including a gold and a bronze at the CBSE National Gymnastics Competition. See our [achievements](/achievements/).

</details>
<details>
<summary>Do you run programs in schools?</summary>

Yes. We run recreational gymnastics programs in schools and are associated with schools across Bengaluru. Visit our [For Schools page](/schools/) to learn more.

</details>
<details>
<summary>Where are you located?</summary>

We are on Kembathalli Main Road, Gottigere, Bengaluru 560083.

</details>
<details>
<summary>What are the class timings?</summary>

We run classes every day of the week, with separate batches for ages 3–7, 8–12, 12–18 and adults. See the full [class timings](/about/#class-timings) on our About page.

</details>

</details>
